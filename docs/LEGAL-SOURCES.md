# OpenStrata Legal Source Library

**Purpose:** Primary-source index for OpenStrata legal workflows  
**Status:** Research baseline; counsel review required before production use  
**Last checked:** 2026-08-25

## How OpenStrata must use this library

- [ ] Link to the original publisher wherever possible.
- [ ] Store the exact title, citation, jurisdiction, source type, URL, effective date, last checked date, and reviewer.
- [ ] Preserve historical and point-in-time versions.
- [ ] Never treat a search result, blog, AI answer, or association article as the law itself.
- [ ] Label legislation, regulations, prescribed forms, government guidance, tribunal decisions, court decisions, and commentary separately.
- [ ] Show a legal-content status: research, awaiting review, counsel-reviewed, published, superseded, or withdrawn.
- [ ] Show “This is general information, not legal advice” wherever plain-language explanations appear.
