# Privacy Policy — OpenStrata

**Status:** Draft — reflects the current static site. A full privacy impact
assessment and expanded policy are required before the backend phases handle
real community data (see `docs/PRODUCT-BUILD-PLAN.md` §16).
**Last updated:** 2026-08-25
**Contact:** hello@giveabit.io

## What we collect today

The current site is a static marketing/educational website. We do not operate
user accounts and do not process strata records.

- **None of the dashboard data is real.** Units, treasury figures, tenants,
  RSS items, and jobs are clearly synthetic demo content for illustration.
- **Local preferences only.** Your language choice is stored in your browser's
  `localStorage` and never leaves your device.
- **Anonymous analytics** may be collected by our hosting/CDN provider
  (Cloudflare) in the ordinary course of serving the site.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell or rent personal information.
- We do not collect payment information — donation buttons are links to
  external wallets/email; we never see credentials.
- We do not process, store, or transmit real strata, financial, or legal
  records on this site.

## BC legal context

OpenStrata provides workflow information and general guidance for BC strata
corporations. It is software and general information — not legal, financial,
or professional advice. When the product adds community workspaces, BC PIPA
obligations, strata records access rules (SPA s.35), and data-retention
schedules will apply; a reviewed privacy policy will accompany those phases.
